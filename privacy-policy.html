Job Radar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Job Radar is a personal, non-commercial project. You fill in a short form and it emails you a list of remote job listings that match your skills. This page explains what happens to the information you submit.

What we collect

  • Your email address
  • The industry, role type and skills you enter
  • A resume PDF, only if you choose to upload one

How it is used

  • Your skills (typed, and extracted from your resume if you upload one) are matched against public remote job listings.
  • If you upload a resume, its contents are sent to Google's Gemini API to extract your skills and to write a short note about each matching job. Google's own terms apply to that processing.
  • Your matches are emailed to the address you entered, sent from a Gmail account that belongs to this project.

What is stored

Job Radar does not keep a database of submissions. Your details are used while your request is processed and are not saved by the project afterwards. The email that is sent to you remains in the project's Gmail sent folder and in your own inbox.

Sharing

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone, apart from the services needed to run the project: Cloudflare (hosting), Google (Gemini and Gmail) and the public job listing sources used to find matches.

Your choices

The resume upload is optional. To ask for the email sent to you to be deleted from the project's sent folder, contact the address below.
