Job Radar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Job Radar is a small personal project, run by one person, that emails you a short list of job listings that match your search. This page explains what happens to the information you submit.

What you give us

  • Your email address
  • Your search: industry, roles, remote or in-person, city or country, experience level, expected salary, and skills
  • A resume PDF, only if you choose to upload one

Your resume and Google's Gemini AI

If you upload a resume, it is sent to Google's Gemini AI to read your skills, years of experience and email address. Job Radar uses Google's free Gemini tier. Under Google's terms for that tier, Google may use content sent to it to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. For that reason the form asks you to agree before you can upload a resume.

The resume is optional. If you are not comfortable with this, do not upload one. You can type your skills and choose your experience level yourself and the service works the same way. Job Radar does not keep your resume.

Gemini is also used to write a short note about why each job suits you. For that step it receives your skills and the text of the job listings, not your resume.

How your search is used

  • Your role, city or country and skills are used to search public job listings and to rank them.
  • The job sources receive only search terms such as the role and city. They do not receive your email address, your skills or your resume.
  • Your matches are emailed to the address you entered, from a Gmail account that belongs to this project.
  • The email includes links that open the same search on other job sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What is kept

By default Job Radar keeps no record of your search or resume. Your details are used while your request is processed and are not saved afterwards. What may remain:

  • The email we send you stays in your own inbox and in the project's Gmail sent folder, including any private links it contains. The person who runs the project can open that account.
  • To prevent misuse, we may keep a daily usage count linked to a scrambled (hashed) form of your email address and to your network address. It is deleted automatically after about a day.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the service, may keep technical logs such as your network address. Job Radar's own logs record counts and job-source errors, not your email, skills or resume.

If you ask us to remember your jobs

Only if you tick "Remember the jobs you send me" on the form, we save the jobs we email you: the title, company, location, salary where shown, link and date. This is so we can leave out jobs you have already been sent and let you look at the list later.

  • The list is linked to you by a scrambled code (a hash made with a secret key), not by your email address. This is not perfect anonymity: someone who had both the saved list and the secret key could check whether a particular email address is in it.
  • Every email we send you while we hold a list includes private links to view it and to delete it. Anyone who has one of those links can use it, so do not share your emails.
  • Saved jobs are deleted automatically after 60 days. You can delete them sooner with the link in any email.
  • If you do not tick the box, nothing about your search is saved.

Who handles your information

We do not sell your information. It passes only through the services needed to run the project: Cloudflare (hosting and, if you ask us to remember your jobs, the storage for that list), Google (Gemini and Gmail), and the job listing sources used to find matches (Remotive, RemoteOK, Arbeitnow, Himalayas, Jobicy, The Muse, Working Nomads, We Work Remotely and Adzuna).

Staying safe from job scams

Job Radar removes listings with obvious scam signs, but it cannot catch every one. A real employer never charges you to apply and never asks for bank details, OTPs or money to start work.

Your choices

The resume upload and the "remember" option are both optional. To delete your saved jobs, use the link in any email we sent you. To ask for an email to be deleted from the project's sent folder, or for any other question about your information, contact the address below.
